新手园丁的起步节奏

  • 每天先观察花园，再决定要不要动手调整。
  • 先记录光照、浇水和虫害，再决定是否添置更多植物。
  • 每次受挫后记下一条经验，时间久了就会积累成方法。

小而持续的记录最有效

用简短日志记下一张照片、一个进展、一个问题和下一步就够了，这已经能形成真实的生长档案。

Observation prompts

  • What changed since the last photo?
  • Which plant looks stressed first?
  • Is the soil still moist one inch down?
  • What will you check again in 48 hours?

Beginner reminders

  • Start with a few crops you actually want to eat or enjoy seeing.
  • Label varieties early. You will forget faster than you expect.
  • Plants in containers usually need water more often than in-ground beds.
  • One clear note beats a long note you never write.
